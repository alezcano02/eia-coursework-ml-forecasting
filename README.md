# Machine learning and forecasting coursework (Universidad EIA)

- `ml_challenges.ipynb`: decision tree and Naive Bayes to predict who continues studying (2.000 people, 10% positive class), and K-means to group 37 candy bars. The tree gets a precision of 93% and a recall of 75% for the minority class; Naive Bayes has a similar AUC but a precision of only 55%.
- `forecasting_assignment.ipynb`: assignment of the forecasting course. White noise and Ljung-Box tests, moving averages, exponential smoothing, Holt and Holt-Winters on sales data, USD/COP and the Dow Jones, EWMA volatility and VaR, and identification of AR/MA processes with ACF and PACF.

The datasets in `data/` were given by the course.
